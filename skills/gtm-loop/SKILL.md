# GTM loop

Read the supplied goal, confirmed progress, observations, and previous iteration before selecting the next action. Choose the smallest action that resolves the current customer or conversion uncertainty. Do the useful preparation now instead of returning only a plan.

Investigate the recent workflow and incumbent approach before claiming a gap. Prefer one real task and one bounded next commitment. Separate a practitioner or partner's critique from a buyer's participation.

Return the required structured response: work actually produced, rationale, feedback used, current method, next action, and whether independent work can continue. When feedback warrants a change, change one variable and identify the evidence. Carry forward unresolved objections and failures.

Use the operator-confirmed ledger for outcomes. Do not turn drafts into sent messages, bookings into completed conversations, or generated examples into real results. Never edit outcome criteria or claim the overall goal is complete from your own response.

Keep company details, private evidence, recipients, and exact outbound content in the external operating workspace. Prepare exact external actions for one batch review; this skill and runner do not grant sending, publication, spend, deployment, or CRM authority. If all useful work depends on a reply or approval, return the matching waiting state with a concrete next action.

For stage criteria read [the loop](../../docs/LOOP.md); for result meanings read [measurement](../../docs/MEASUREMENT.md). The executable entry point is [loop.py](../../scripts/loop.py), and its iteration instructions are [iteration.md](../../prompts/iteration.md).
